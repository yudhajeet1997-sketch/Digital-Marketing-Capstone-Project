Campaign Overview & Execution Report
To launch Adidas's new football boots and scale global brand engagement across key digital channels, I executed a multi-channel digital marketing strategy comprising YouTube Video Ad Sequencing, an emotion-led Social Media Strategy, and segmented Email Campaigns.

Strategy Breakdown
1. YouTube Marketing: The 3-Step "Blitz" Ad Sequence
To move high-intent football enthusiasts (ages 14–34) through a structured sales funnel quickly, I deployed a sequential video ad campaign targeting fans of top Adidas athletes and in-market sporting goods shoppers.

Step 1: The Teaser (Unveil) | 6-Second Bumper Ad: Focused on slow-motion close-ups of shoe texture colliding with a football to build intrigue. Viewing this ad triggered audience progression to Step 2.

Step 2: The Reveal (The Hook) | 15-Second Non-Skippable Ad: Features a premier Adidas athlete lacing up and executing a game-winning move to showcase core product benefits ("Unmatched Speed" / "Absolute Control").

Step 3: The Push (Action) | 6-Second Bumper Ad: Displays fast cuts of various colorways alongside "Evolution is Here" text and a prominent "Shop Now" companion banner directing traffic directly to the Adidas store.

2. Social Media Marketing: Instagram & Facebook
Anchored in the "Impossible is Nothing" ethos, the social strategy prioritized emotional storytelling and street culture over basic product specs.

Instagram: Leveraged Reels (high-velocity transitions, trending audio), interactive Stories (polls, drop countdowns, link stickers), and native Collab posts with Tier-1 athletes to double reach.

Facebook: Focused on long-form mini-documentaries highlighting community sports initiatives and direct-in-app sales via Facebook Shops catalog integration.

Content Execution & Growth: Deployed a structured weekly content rollout—spanning early-morning athlete training montages, sustainable material carousels, UGC unboxing reshares, live designer Q&As, and streetwear styling guides. Hosted a "Create Your Fit" engagement challenge to drive high-volume viral UGC.

3. Targeted Email Marketing Campaigns
I tailored two distinct lifecycle email tracks to align with specific audience motivations:

Performance Running (Ultraboost): Targeted marathoners and daily joggers, focusing on technology, energy return, and long-distance comfort.

Adidas Originals (Samba / Gazelle): Targeted Gen Z and sneakerheads, leveraging heritage styling, lifestyle versatility, and exclusive drop scarcity (FOMO).
